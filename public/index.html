Advisory services

Four ways we work with your lab.

Start with one, or combine them. Each engagement is scoped in writing before any work begins.

Flagship engagement

Laboratory Quality & Performance Diagnostic

A focused review of selected ISO/IEC 17025 quality-system practices, method-control records and operational indicators. You receive an evidence-linked findings register, prioritized action plan and management readout.

Scope, methods, applicable standards editions and fees are confirmed in writing before work begins. This is an independent advisory review, not an accreditation assessment.

Discuss a diagnostic
01

Quality-system assessments

A structured review of your management system and technical records against ISO/IEC 17025, ahead of an accreditation, reassessment or customer audit.

Typical deliverables
  • Gap assessment mapped to the clauses of the standard
  • Prioritized findings with the evidence behind each one
  • Review of documented information, traceability and internal audit records
  • Corrective-action plan with suggested owners and sequence
02

Method and QC support

Help with the technical side of your scope: how each method is verified, how its uncertainty is estimated, and how routine QC shows the method stays in control.

Typical deliverables
  • Method verification plans and records for ASTM methods such as D4052, D86 and D445
  • Measurement uncertainty budgets and worked examples
  • Control charts, QC rules and check-standard schedules
  • Review of proficiency-testing and interlaboratory results
03

Operational intelligence

Turn the records your lab already keeps in a LIMS or in spreadsheets into measures a lab manager can act on, without changing how samples are tested.

Typical deliverables
  • Turnaround time, workload and backlog reporting
  • QC trend and out-of-control summaries by method and instrument
  • Calibration, reference-material and training due-date tracking
  • Nonconformance and corrective-action trend reports
04

Implementation assistance

Hands-on help to close the findings: drafting, revising and rolling out the procedures, records and routines your lab will actually follow.

Typical deliverables
  • Procedures, forms and record templates fitted to your lab
  • Document control, calibration and equipment-record setup
  • Staff walk-throughs on the new procedures and records
  • Follow-up check that the changes are being used

Questions quality managers ask.

About advisory services

Will an assessment get my lab accredited?

No. Accreditation is decided by your accreditation body, based on how your lab actually operates. An assessment shows you where your system stands against the standard and what to fix first. It does not guarantee any outcome.

Are you an accreditation body or an assessor?

No. OmniServe Labs is an advisory service. It is not an accreditation body, certification body or standards organization, and it is not affiliated with ISO, IEC, ASTM or any accreditation body.

Can you work on site?

Yes, across the Texas Gulf Coast, including the Houston, Beaumont and Corpus Christi areas. Remote reviews are available for labs elsewhere. Tell us your location in the inquiry.

How is the work priced?

Each engagement is quoted after the scoping call, with scope, deliverables and fee set out in the written proposal. You approve it before any work starts.

Will you see our confidential data?

Only what the engagement needs. We can agree confidentiality terms before you share records, and we ask that you keep sample results and customer names out of the inquiry form.

About digital products

Will the workbooks and kits get my lab accredited?

No document can guarantee accreditation. They give your quality system a starting structure to adapt and verify; your accreditation body assesses how your lab implements it, and your lab remains responsible for its own validation and procedures.

Can my company pay with an invoice or purchase order?

Online checkout through Payhip gives an immediate receipt. For a purchase order or invoice, email us and we will arrange it.

Can I get a refund?

Because the products are digital and delivered instantly, all sales are final. Start with the free workbook to see the quality of the work before you buy.
